# VaradIQ — Global launch kit

**The plan:** one big launch day on **Product Hunt**, backed by posts on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, plus messages to friends. The aim is early-access sign-ups from around the world.

**Be honest about where we are:** places, prices and bills are sample data for now. Launch it as **"early access"**: the AI is real, and live data is coming. Product Hunt users respect honesty and dislike hype.

## Do's and don'ts

- ✅ **Ask people to "check it out and share feedback"**, not to "upvote me". Product Hunt penalises vote-begging.
- ✅ **Reply to every comment quickly**, especially in the first 4 hours.
- ✅ **Post from your own accounts**, with your real story.
- ❌ **Don't buy upvotes or use voting groups.** Product Hunt detects them and can hide your launch.
- ❌ **Don't post the same link in many Reddit or Facebook groups.** Many ban self-promotion, so read each group's rules first.
- ❌ **Don't promise features that aren't built** (e.g. "books for you"). Keep to early access, real AI and sample data.
