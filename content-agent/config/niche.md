# Niche brief

**Audience:** non-technical small business owners (trades, local shops, cafes,
freelancers, small agencies) who keep hearing about AI tools but don't have
time to evaluate them.

**Promise of the newsletter:** one practical, no-hype AI tool or workflow
explained per week — what it does, who it's actually worth it for, what it
costs, and how to try it in under 10 minutes.

**Tone:** plain language, skeptical of hype, concrete examples over theory.
No jargon without an example. Assume the reader runs a business, not a tech
team.

**What to avoid:**
- Generic "10 AI tools you must try" listicles — pick ONE tool/workflow per
  issue and go deep.
- Overclaiming ("this will 10x your business") — be honest about limits.
- Enterprise/dev-focused tools — this is for solo owners and small teams.

**Monetization:**
- Affiliate/referral links to the tool(s) covered, where a legitimate
  program exists (disclose clearly in every post).
- Long-term: a paid "AI toolkit for small business" guide, once there's an
  audience worth selling to (see content-agent/README.md phase plan).

**Call to action in every post:** subscribe for next week's tool, plus one
line inviting replies with questions — this is how you'd learn what readers
actually want covered next.
